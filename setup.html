Amazon FireStick & Android TV Setup

Recommended App: IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate IPTV Player

Estimated Time: 3 Mins
1

Install "Downloader" App

On your FireStick home screen, go to Find > Search, type Downloader, and click download/install.

💡 Tip: Enable Developer Options under Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options > Install unknown apps.
2

Enter Downloader Code

Open Downloader, click the URL bar, type one of our direct numeric shortcodes below, and click Go:

78522
Installs IPTV Smarters Pro 4K
3

Log in with Xtream API

Open IPTV Smarters, select "Login with Xtream Codes API", and enter the details from your activation email/WhatsApp:

Name: Luna Stream
Server URL: http://line.lunaentertainment.online:8080
Username: [Your Email/User]
Password: [Your Password]
Need assistance? Our engineers can guide you live over chat.

Samsung Tizen & LG WebOS Smart TV Setup

Recommended Apps: IBO Player, BOB Player, or Smart IPTV

Estimated Time: 4 Mins
1

Install IBO Player

Open the Samsung App Store or LG Content Store on your Smart TV, search for IBO Player, and install it.

2

Find Your Device MAC & Key

Open the app on your TV. Note down the Device MAC Address (e.g., aa:bb:cc:11:22:33) and Device Key displayed on your screen.

3

Send Details to Support

Simply forward your MAC and Device Key to our WhatsApp support, and we will upload the 20,000+ channels and VOD directly into your TV in 60 seconds!

You can also upload your playlist yourself at iboplayer.com/device/login
Send TV MAC to WhatsApp

Apple TV, iPhone & iPad (iOS) Setup

Recommended Apps: Smarters Player Lite, GSE Smart IPTV, or IPTVX

Estimated Time: 2 Mins
1

Install from App Store

Open the official iOS or Apple TV App Store and download "Smarters Player Lite" or "IPTVX".

2

Choose Login Method

Launch the application and select "Add User" → "Login with Xtream Codes API".

3

Enter Credentials & Enjoy

Input your Server URL, Username, and Password provided in your subscription email, then click Add User.

📟

MAG 250/254/322/420 & Enigma2 Setup

Portal Protocol: Stalker Middleware

Estimated Time: 3 Mins
1

Open MAG Settings

Go to Settings > System Settings > Servers > Portals on your MAG box remote control.

2

Enter Portal URL

Set Portal 1 Name to Luna Stream and enter the portal URL:

http://mag.lunaentertainment.online:8080/c/
3

Register MAG MAC

Send your MAC address (starting with 00:1A:79:... located underneath the MAG box) to our WhatsApp/Telegram support.

Windows PC & MacOS Laptop Setup

Recommended: IPTV Smarters Desktop or VLC Media Player

Estimated Time: 2 Mins
1

Download Player App

Download IPTV Smarters Pro .exe for Windows or .dmg for Mac, or use VLC Media Player.

2

Load M3U or Xtream API

In VLC: Click Media > Open Network Stream and paste your M3U link. In Smarters: enter your server URL and login credentials.

3

Browser Web Player

You can also stream directly in Google Chrome, Edge, or Safari without installing anything using our web player portal.

Pro Tips

Troubleshooting & Optimization

Some ISPs throttle streaming servers during live sports. You can resolve this instantly by changing your router DNS to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) or Google DNS (8.8.8.8), or enabling a VPN.
In IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate, go to Settings > Stream Format and change it from Default / TS to HLS (.m3u8). This enhances buffer stability on slower Wi-Fi connections.
