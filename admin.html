💡 How Dynamic Pricing & Promotion Rules Work

  • Sale Price: The actual price charged to customer upon checkout. Authoritative server verification strictly enforces this price.
  • Cut / Previous Price (Strikethrough): The crossed-out comparison price (e.g. £85.00). If set higher than sale price, the customer sees a calculated discount percentage pill (e.g. -24% OFF). Leave equal or empty to show regular price without strikethrough.
  • Bonus Extra Months: Additional free months added to the plan. E.g. setting 2 extra months on the 12-Month plan displays +2 Months Free on the pricing page, and automatically grants a 14-month subscription in the IPTV panel upon order completion.
